Noor Connect is a comprehensive, privacy-first Islamic companion — Quran, prayer times, Qibla, hadith, tafsir and more — with zero ads, zero trackers and zero accounts. Everything you use stays on your own device.

<b>Quran, Tafsir &amp; Reading</b>
• Mushaf reader with 600+ page images and authentic page layout
• 26 tafsir editions (Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Qurtubi, Jalalayn…) — read online, or download any edition for fully offline reading
• Khatma progress tracking, bookmarks and reading streaks

<b>Prayer Times &amp; Adhan</b>
• 13 calculation methods (MWL, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Gulf, Moonsighting, Turkey…) with Hanafi / Shafi'i Asr
• Adhan alarms that survive reboot, a different adhan per prayer, and your own uploaded adhan audio
• Pre-prayer, post-prayer and prayer-end reminders
• Hijri calendar with manual adjustment for local moon sighting

<b>Qibla</b>
• Precision compass with sensor calibration, manual bearing mode and distance to the Kaaba

<b>Hadith &amp; Knowledge</b>
• 16 authentic collections (~52,000 hadiths): Bukhari, Muslim, Nasai, Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah, Mishkat, Malik's Muwatta and more — fully offline, searchable
• Daily ayah and daily hadith with reflections

<b>Noor Cycle</b>
• Fiqh-aware menstrual tracker that pauses prayer reminders during your cycle
• 50 fiqh Q&As with sources, pregnancy mode, ghusl checklist and cycle predictions

<b>Library &amp; Media</b>
• Stories of the Prophets (Qisas) and a 33-chapter Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ
• 200+ Islamic e-books from Archive.org with offline reading
• 24/7 live Makkah &amp; Madinah streams, Quran radio, recitation audio and copyright-free nasheeds

<b>More</b>
• Zakat and Fitrana calculators with live gold/silver rates
• Qaza tracker, habit tracker, digital tasbeeh and a gamified Islamic quiz
• 8 languages: English, العربية, اردو, বাংলা, Русский, Français, 한국어, Türkçe

<b>Privacy First</b>
No analytics, no trackers, no ads, no accounts. All data — prayer records, bookmarks, cycle logs, habits — stays on your device, protected by automatic local backups and Android's own backup system. Fully open source under GPL-3.0.
